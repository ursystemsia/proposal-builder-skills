# Upwork proposal check

Last reviewed: 2026-10-26.

Before a contract begins, keep communication on Upwork and remove personal contact information from the proposal. Portfolio or prior-work links may be shared when relevant, but the proposal should make clear that communication stays on Upwork until a contract starts. Limited exceptions exist for Enterprise clients and certain legal or system-access needs; do not assume an exception.

Check for:

- email addresses, phone numbers, social handles, messaging usernames, or invitations to communicate elsewhere;
- requests for off-platform payment or purchases required to apply;
- suspicious links, credential requests, or unnecessary access;
- unsupported claims, generic text, and promises not backed by the freelancer's evidence;
- unanswered screening questions, if supplied with the job post.

Official sources:

- [How to keep your contact information safe on Upwork](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051749534-How-to-keep-your-contact-information-safe-on-Upwork)
- [How to share documents, files, and more on Upwork](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049608113-How-to-share-documents-files-and-more-on-Upwork)

Recheck the official Help Center when a proposal depends on an exception or current platform behavior.
