# Workana proposal check

Last reviewed: 2026-10-26.

Keep communication and payment within Workana. Do not include personal contact information. Portfolio presentation rules distinguish between prohibited contact-bearing links and some work samples without contact details, so verify the current Help Center before including any external link.

Check for:

- email addresses, phone numbers, social or messaging profiles, meeting links, or off-platform payment;
- portfolio files or links containing contact details, including third-party contact information;
- free work, unjustifiably low trial proposals, generic repeated text, or payment based only on commission or equity;
- unclear deliverables, milestones, revisions, timeline, payment conditions, or material assumptions;
- a need to ask questions through the project's messages before committing to price or schedule.

Official sources:

- [Workana policies](https://help.workana.com/hc/es/articles/360040841214-Pol%C3%ADticas-de-Workana)
- [How to show a portfolio](https://help.workana.com/hc/es/articles/360041612974--C%C3%B3mo-muestro-mi-portfolio)
- [How to apply for a project](https://help.workana.com/hc/es/articles/360041477614--C%C3%B3mo-postularte-a-un-proyecto-en-Workana)

Recheck the official Help Center when deciding whether a specific attachment or link is allowed.
